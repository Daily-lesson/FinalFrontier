# Playtest — the fun gate (Gate 1)

The form for prototype 3. One page, answerable from a phone. The protocol and
the pass rule are the design's own (alys `docs/fieldworks/GAME_DEVELOPMENT.md`
§5); this file is where they are kept to hand.

**Before each session.** Open the prototype link with nothing due. Leave the dev
bar alone except the first time (Clock: *Real day* if you want the night at
21:00; *Parts act* stays off unless you are testing it). Play until you want to
stop. Then answer the five lines below, one line each, before you do anything
else.

## The five questions

1. **Did you want to open it, or did you make yourself?**
2. **What did you do first, and was it obvious?**
3. **Name the one moment that felt good, and the one that felt like a chore.**
4. **Did anything feel like it was nagging you about your real work?**
5. **Would you open it again tomorrow with nothing due?**

## The three sessions

Copy this block three times, one per session, on three different days.

```
Session N · date
1. wanted / made myself — 
2. first thing — 
3. good — ; chore — 
4. nagged? yes / no — 
5. again tomorrow? yes / no — 
Minutes (Keeper → Your numbers): 
```

## The pass rule

**Gate 1 passes when question 1 and question 5 are yes on at least two of the
three sessions, and question 4 is no on all three.** Anything else is a design
note, not a failure: it goes into the next round as a change to the rules or
the prototype, checked against the economy sim (`sim/REPORT.md`) first.

## Where the answers go

A line each in the next alys pipeline ledger entry (alys `pipeline/ledger/`),
under the run that reads them, as the owner writes them. Nothing about a
session goes into this repository: it is public. The local numbers on the
Keeper screen stay on the device; quote them in the ledger line only if you
choose to.
