# Playtest — the fun gate (Gate 1)

The form for prototype 4 (the rework: the castle, announced hordes, the
stages). One page, answerable from a phone. The protocol and the pass rule are
the design's own (alys `docs/fieldworks/GAME_DEVELOPMENT.md` §5); this file is
where they are kept to hand.

**Before each session.** Open the prototype link with nothing due. Play until
you want to stop. Then answer the five lines below, one line each, before you
do anything else.

**What there is to do.** The castle is the home screen; it is built by the
DEMO's finished tasks and it defends itself. A horde is already announced:
finish a task or two from *Finish a task (evidence)* and watch the count under
the countdown fall. Step the horde clock from the dev bar (*+1 day* three
times) to meet the horde; fire from the wall or do nothing. *Out the gate* is
the game: Week 1, then the next once it is cleared, or the Wilds for as long
as the hull lasts. Left, right, jump, fire: the four buttons, or arrows, space
and J.

## The five questions

1. **Did you want to open it, or did you make yourself?**
2. **What did you do first, and was it obvious?**
3. **Name the one moment that felt good, and the one that felt like a chore.**
4. **Did anything feel like it was nagging you about your real work?**
5. **Would you open it again tomorrow with nothing due?**

Three more, for this round only, because the rework's shape is still
proposed. A word each is enough.

6. **The stages: fun on their own, or filler?**
7. **The warning (the countdown and "2 finished tasks would see it hold"):
   a push to finish something, or a nag?**
8. **The horde: worth watching, or would you skip it every time?**

## The three sessions

Copy this block three times, one per session, on three different days.

```
Session N · date
1. wanted / made myself — 
2. first thing — 
3. good — ; chore — 
4. nagged? yes / no — 
5. again tomorrow? yes / no — 
6. stages — 
7. warning — 
8. horde — 
Minutes (Keeper → Your numbers): 
```

## The pass rule

**Gate 1 passes when question 1 and question 5 are yes on at least two of the
three sessions, and question 4 is no on all three.** Anything else is a design
note, not a failure: it goes into the next round as a change to the rules or
the prototype, checked against the economy sim (`sim/REPORT.md`) first.
Questions 6 to 8 do not count toward the gate; they say which of the proposals
to keep.

## Where the answers go

A line each in the next alys pipeline ledger entry (alys `pipeline/ledger/`),
under the run that reads them, as the owner writes them. Nothing about a
session goes into this repository: it is public. The local numbers on the
Keeper screen stay on the device; quote them in the ledger line only if you
choose to.
